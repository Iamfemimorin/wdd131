SPOT THE WARNING SIGNS

Know the threats. Choose safer actions.

Online threats often rely on urgency, curiosity, or trust. Learn the common patterns so you can pause, check, and respond deliberately.

COMMON ONLINE RISKS

Threats to recognize

Filter the cards by topic to focus on what you want to learn.

01

Phishing

Fraudulent messages imitate a trusted person or organization to trick you into clicking a link, opening an attachment, or sharing information.

Safer response: Check the sender and destination carefully. Visit the official website directly instead of using an unexpected link.

02

Malware

Malicious software can steal information, disrupt a device, or give an attacker access. It may arrive through unsafe downloads or attachments.

Safer response: Install software from trusted sources, keep protection tools updated, and avoid unexpected files.

03

Password attacks

Weak or reused passwords can make it easier for someone to access more than one account if a password is exposed.

Safer response: Use long, unique passwords and consider a reputable password manager.

04

Online scams

Scammers may promise prizes, urgent refunds, jobs, or investment returns to pressure people into sending money or details.

Safer response: Slow down, verify the claim independently, and never pay a fee to claim a surprise prize.

05

Unpatched software

Out-of-date operating systems, browsers, and apps may contain known security weaknesses.

Safer response: Turn on automatic updates where appropriate and remove apps you no longer use.

06

Identity theft

Personal information can be misused to impersonate you, access accounts, or attempt financial fraud.

Safer response: Share less personal information publicly and review account activity and security alerts.

PRACTICAL PROTECTION

Build these everyday habits

No single step removes every risk. Layering simple habits improves your protection.

✓

Turn on multifactor authentication

Where available, use an additional verification step beyond your password. Prefer stronger options offered by the service, such as an authenticator app or security key.

✓

Use unique passwords

A password manager can help you create and store different passwords, reducing the impact if one password is exposed.

✓

Update your devices

Apply operating system, browser, and application updates so known weaknesses can be fixed.

✓

Back up important files

Keep a backup of important information in a separate location and check that you can restore it.

✓

Pause before sharing

Be cautious with urgent requests, unexpected attachments, public Wi-Fi, and requests for sensitive details.
